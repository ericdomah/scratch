# Academic Search Queries for GridGuard AI Thesis

Use these search strings across Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, and arXiv to find supporting literature and comparative baselines.

## 1. Boolean Search Strings (For Professional Databases)
*Use these in IEEE Xplore, Scopus, or Web of Science.*

- **Architecture Focus:** `("Electricity Theft" OR "Non-Technical Loss") AND ("Meta-Ensemble" OR "Hybrid Model") AND ("LSTM" AND "Transformer")`
- **Context-Aware Focus:** `("Electricity Theft Detection") AND ("Context-Aware" OR "Grid Topology" OR "Substation Load") AND "Smart Grid"`
- **XAI Focus:** `("Explainable AI" OR "XAI") AND ("Integrated Gradients" OR "SHAP") AND ("Energy Fraud" OR "Theft Detection")`
- **Data Augmentation Focus:** `("Digital Twin" OR "Synthetic Data") AND "Electricity Theft" AND ("Data Augmentation" OR "Physics-based")`
- **Deployment Focus:** `("Edge Computing" OR "Cloud Cascade") AND "Smart Meter" AND "Real-time Detection" AND "Electricity Theft"`

## 3. Recommended Academic Databases
1. **IEEE Xplore:** Primary source for Electrical Engineering and Grid logic.
2. **ScienceDirect (Elsevier):** Focused on Energy Policy and Power Systems.
3. **ACM Digital Library:** Best for the Deep Learning and Transformer modeling.
4. **arXiv.org (Computer Science - Learning):** Best for the most recent pre-prints (2024+).
5. **MDPI Energies:** Highly active journal for smart grid security.

## 4. Citation Strategy Tip
When you find a paper that is similar to GridGuard, check their **"Future Work"** section. If they mention a limitation that you have already solved (like "lack of explainability" or "false positive issues"), cite them and highlight how your work addresses that specific gap.
